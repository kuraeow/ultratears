branding below is not**: it identifies this project, and the GPL does not cover it.

Branding:

- the name **UltraTears** and the short name **ultrs** as the name of a program, port or launcher;
- the emblem and the icon: `assets/icon.ico`, `assets/launcher/l_emblem.png`;
- the cover: `assets/cover.png`;
- the launcher's background pattern: `assets/launcher/l_pattern.png`.

A fork may of course keep the code under the GPL; it only has to look and be named like itself. For a modified
version, replace `assets/icon.ico` (the game's build compiles it into `UltraTears.exe`) and the launcher's images in
`assets/launcher/`.
